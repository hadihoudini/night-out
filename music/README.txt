Drop real music tracks in this folder to replace the built-in chiptune for that situation.
Any track you don't add keeps playing the built-in music, so you can add them one at a time.

File names (mp3, ogg or wav):

  flat.mp3     Pre-drinks at the flat: upbeat, excited
  bar.mp3      The bar: chilled, groovy
  club.mp3     The club dance floor: energetic dance/house
  tense.mp3    Spiking and being followed: tense, pulsing, uneasy
  street.mp3   Street at night / ambulance / taxi: quiet, eerie, lonely
  end.mp3      Ending screen: hopeful, relieved

Tracks loop, so seamless loops work best. Keep each under ~5 MB.
The game still muffles and slows them when a character is spiked.

Licence-safe places to get free tracks (check each track's licence and credit the artist on Devpost):
  - OpenGameArt.org: search "chiptune" and filter by CC0 or CC-BY
  - Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music): free for commercial and non-commercial use
  - freesound.org: for loops and ambience, check the licence on each sound
